Questions

Quick answers before you run the script.

How do I hide the menu?

Press RightShift. You can pick another key in Settings.

My game isn’t listed. What happens?

You still get the Other tab and the ScriptBlox browser. Tell us the game in Discord or in the Feedback tab and we’ll look at adding it.

Are Anti-AFK and auto rejoin on by default?

No. Both start off. Turn them on in Settings, System.

Which languages does the menu support?

Tiếng Việt, English and Português (Brasil). Change it in Settings.

Where do I report a bug?

Use the Feedback tab in the menu or write in our Discord.
